# Money Manager v2.0 Production Release Notes

**Release Date**: November 14, 2026  
**Build Target**: Android (Production Release Ready)

---

## What's New in Version 2.0

### 1. Mathematical Accuracy & Precision
- Completely overhauled core balance engine to use exact integer minor units (paise/cents).
- Rounding artifacts and fractional penny errors have been permanently eliminated.

### 2. Account Archiving & Historical Preservation
- You can now archive accounts you no longer actively use without losing your historical ledger data.
- Accounts with existing transactions are safely protected against accidental deletion.

### 3. Dedicated Credit Card Features
- Set your credit limit, track available credit in real time, and monitor your statement billing cycle and due date.
- Paying off your credit card bill no longer double-counts as an expense against your monthly budget.

### 4. Smarter Quick-Add Templates
- Quick templates in the Ledger now clearly display the destination account, amount, and category before you log them.
- Tapping a template opens the transaction form pre-filled so you can review details before confirming.

### 5. Money Map Daily Spending Calendar
- Track your safe-to-spend allowance on a daily basis.
- Under-spending rolls over automatically to future days, and over-spending adjusts your remaining daily allowance.
- Switch seamlessly between Calendar Grid and Agenda List views.

### 6. "Deduct from Budget" Toggle
- Reimbursable work expenses, capital purchases, or debt repayments can now be excluded from your monthly budget tracking with a single toggle, while remaining part of your overall financial analytics.

### 7. Automated Monthly PDF Reports
- On the 1st of every month, Money Manager automatically compiles two comprehensive PDF reports:
  1. *Monthly Income & Expense Analysis*
  2. *Monthly Budget Performance & Rollover Report*
- Browse past reports, view SHA-256 integrity checksums, and share them directly from the app.
